Creator OS

A personal Mac app for planning and learning from YouTube videos.

Creator OS is a private desktop tool used by one YouTube creator to develop video ideas, plan their packaging and structure, and learn from each video's results. It runs entirely on the creator's own Mac.

It connects to the creator's own YouTube channel with read-only permission to show that channel's analytics, and reads public YouTube data for channels the creator chooses to follow. It is not offered to other users.
